Privacy Policy

Okibi app
日本語

This page explains how the iPhone app Okibi (“the app”) handles information about the people who use it. The app is provided by Yoshihiko Okano, an independent developer (“the developer”).

1. We don’t collect personal information

The app has no account or sign-in. The developer does not collect your name, email address, phone number, location, or any other personal information.

2. What stays on your iPhone

Your settings, the things you find, how you decorate the campsite, and whether you have made a purchase are stored only on your iPhone. They are never sent to the developer or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes this data.

3. Information sent to receive app updates

To receive program updates, the app communicates with servers run by Expo (650 Industries, Inc., USA). When it does, the following technical information is sent:

  • A random identifier created for each installation, which does not identify you
  • Information such as the app version and the update in use
  • Error details if the app closes unexpectedly
  • The IP address needed for the connection

This information is used only to deliver updates correctly and to find problems. It contains no personal information such as your name, and it is never used for advertising or tracking. For how Expo handles it, see Expo’s Privacy Policy.

4. Purchases

In-app purchases are made through Apple’s App Store. Apple handles your payment information; the developer never receives it. For details, see Apple’s Privacy Policy.

5. No ads, analytics, or tracking

The app contains no advertising, no usage analytics, and nothing that tracks you across other apps or websites.

6. Features we don’t use

The app does not use your location, microphone, camera, contacts, photos, or health data. It does not measure sleep or heart rate.

7. Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, of any age.

8. Contact

If you have questions about this policy, please email r451mjkl@icloud.com. Your email address and message are used only to reply to you.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the update will be posted on this page.

Effective date: October 5, 2026
