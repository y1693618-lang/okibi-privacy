Frequently asked questions

I can’t hear the campfire

In the app’s Settings, check that “Campfire sound” is on. Change the volume with your iPhone’s volume buttons while the sound is playing (it isn’t affected by the ringer volume or Silent mode). The sound fades out when the time chosen in “Sound timer” has passed.

It doesn’t vibrate

Vibration happens only during “Time to unwind” and stops once “Bedtime mode” begins. In the app’s Settings, check that “Vibration” is on, and in your iPhone’s Settings, check that Sounds & Haptics > System Haptics is on.

The morning screen doesn’t appear

After the time set in “Morning starts at” (5:00 by default), open the app or touch the screen and the morning screen appears. You can also touch the screen in bedtime mode and tap “Go to morning.”

The season isn’t right

Seasons follow the calendar. The hemisphere is decided by your iPhone’s Region setting. If it doesn’t match, choose “Northern Hemisphere” or “Southern Hemisphere” in the app’s Settings > Seasons.

I don’t want decorations at the campsite

Turn off “Campsite decorations” in the app’s Settings, and nothing is displayed at the night campsite. Turn it back on and everything returns to where it was.

I’d like less motion

In the app’s Settings, set “Flame motion” to “Gentle.” Turning on Reduce Motion in your iPhone’s Settings > Accessibility > Motion stops moving effects such as sparks and falling leaves.

The screen turns off / stays on

Whether the screen turns off follows your iPhone’s Auto-Lock setting. The campfire sound keeps playing with the screen off.

What is free?

The campfire, sounds, breathing time, and timer are free forever. Finding and decorating is free for your first 7 nights. To keep going, get “All Seasons,” a one-time purchase that includes all four seasons and any seasons added later.

I got a new iPhone and want my purchase back

Tap “Restore Purchase” at the bottom of the app’s Settings. You need to be signed in with the same Apple ID you used to buy it.

About this app
